Method

The value model (radar-2)

Every row is scored by the same formula, from the snapshot's own figures only. No protocol name is special-cased.

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chain reach

  • Category multiple — the median circulating market-cap per dollar of TVL among the live-token protocols in the same category (at least 3 comparables, else the global median), clamped to 0.05–0.60. It is recomputed from the snapshot on every run.
  • Growth — 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −60%, +120%) ÷ 100 × 0.5.
  • Maturity — 0.6 + min(age in days, 1095) ÷ 1095 × 0.4. A protocol with no listing date is scored as 365 days old.
  • Chain reach — 1 + min(chains − 1, 4) × 0.03.

Who is on the board

A protocol is ranked when it records no token (no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol) and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades: no CEX, Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking or Restaked BTC category, no whole word “wrapped”, “staked” or “staking” in its name, no exchange-prefixed name, no versioned name such as “Foo V2”. The 40-protocol watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling: this board ranks every rankable protocol in the snapshot.

Farm plans

Each protocol's six steps are generated from its own record — category, main chain, TVL and age. They are a positioning checklist, not financial advice, and they never promise a token: a protocol that records no token today may never issue one.
